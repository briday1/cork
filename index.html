MAKE YOURSELF AT HOME

Thoughts don’t have to line up.

Choose a category from the sidebar, then click a spot on the board. Your note is ready to write in.

Put it anywhere

Move notes using their top edge. Scroll to zoom around your pointer, or drag empty cork to pan. Pinch to zoom on touch screens.

Make a little room

Notes grow as you type. Use the list button for bullets; Enter continues a list and an empty bullet ends it.

Follow a surprise

“Surprise me” picks from all categories. Press R for a random note, I for an index card, or Esc to cancel.

Keep your thoughts

Your board saves in this browser on this device. Use Export board in the sidebar’s board menu for a backup, or Import board to bring one back.

Categories come from categories.txt in the repository ↗. Edit one category per line and publish; refresh the board to see the changes. Your existing notes stay put.

Bring this board in?

Your current board will be replaced. Export it first if you want to keep both. You can also undo an import.
