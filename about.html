A little room for loose thoughts.

Cork is a free visual board for sticky notes, lined index cards, photos, and connections. Arrange thoughts anywhere, group related ideas, and pan or zoom across the board. Use it to plan a story, organize a project, or collect ideas without forcing them into a task list.

Open Cork

Your browser is the starting place

You can write without creating a Cork account. Boards save in your browser on your device. Download a board as a portable JSON file, keep photos with it, or export a PDF of its arrangement. GitHub Pages hosts the static app; board content is not written to the repository.

Optional Google Drive files

When the site owner enables Google Drive, you can choose to connect your Google account, save individual board files to Drive, and open those files on another device. Cork requests access only to files it creates or that you select for use with Cork. It does not request access to your entire Drive.

Google sign-in and file transfers run directly in your browser. After your first Drive save, board edits save automatically. Your Google account also keeps one editable categories text file, shared across all your boards. Browser saving continues while you work. Drive is optional and can be disconnected. This is file saving, not simultaneous collaboration.

Read the privacy policy for details about browser storage, Google account information, and deleting data.
