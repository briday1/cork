Cork privacy policy

Updated October 25, 2026

Browser storage

Cork stores boards, notes, categories, preferences, and file-link details in your browser's local storage. Original photos, previews, and supported local file or folder handles use IndexedDB on your device. Local folder links do not upload the folder's contents. Cork has no backend database for your boards and does not send board contents to GitHub.

Optional Google Drive access

If Google Drive is enabled and you choose to connect it, Google handles account selection and consent. Cork requests the Google Drive per-file permission (drive.file) to create board files and your account's categories text file, and to read or update files you select for Cork. Cork does not request permission to read your entire Drive.

Cork reads the connected account's display name, email address, and Drive permission identifier to show the connection and check that a saved board belongs to the intended account. The account identifier, Drive file ID, filename, and revision details may be saved locally alongside the board link. Cork keeps the short-lived access token, its expiry, and account identifier in the current tab's sessionStorage so refreshing can restore the connection. Cork verifies the account with Google before resuming Drive saves. Expired or rejected tokens and disconnected sessions are removed. Session storage normally ends when the tab closes; a browser's tab-restoration feature may restore it until the token expires. Cork never stores access or refresh tokens in localStorage, IndexedDB, or board files, and never receives a refresh token or your Google password. If session storage is unavailable, the connection lasts only for the current page.

After you first save or open a Drive board, edits automatically update that board file after a short pause. Files transfer directly between your browser and Google and contain the board's text, positions, appearance, connections, and original photos. Connecting Drive also loads or creates a separate categories text file for your Google account, shared across all your boards. If no account list exists on your first connection, Cork uses the current browser list as its starting content. Editing or importing categories updates this text file automatically. Cork remembers the last categories account, its label, cached text, and file revision in this browser so the list remains available offline. Separate Google accounts have separate cached lists. Local folder permissions and folder contents are not uploaded. Google's authorization library loads when you open the configured Drive integration or Cork restores an unexpired tab session; its picker library loads when you choose another Drive file.

Use and sharing of information

Cork uses Google account and file data only to provide the requested connection, file selection, opening, and saving features. Cork does not sell your information, use your content for advertising or AI model training, or send it to an analytics service. Google processes account information and Drive files under its own policies. GitHub Pages and network providers receive ordinary website connection information when serving the static app.

Cork does not change a file's sharing settings. Anyone who already has access to a selected Drive file may see updates you save to it. Manage file sharing through Google Drive.

Cork's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Retention, disconnecting, and deletion

Browser data remains until you remove it or the browser clears it. Clearing this site's browser data removes its locally stored boards, photos, preferences, and file links. Download anything you need first. You can delete exported local files through your device's file manager.

Disconnecting Drive clears Cork's current authorization and asks Google to revoke the grant when Google is reachable. You can also remove Cork's access through your Google account's connected-app settings. Disconnecting does not delete board files, the account's categories file, or cached browser copies. Delete Drive files and manage their trash or revision history in Google Drive. Clearing this site's browser data also removes its cached categories and remembered categories account.

Contact

For questions or privacy requests, contact the maintainer through the Cork repository. Issues are public; do not include private board content or account credentials.
