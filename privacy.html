Cork privacy policy

Updated October 25, 2026

Browser storage

Cork stores boards, notes, categories, preferences, and file-link details in your browser's local storage. Original photos, previews, and supported local file or folder handles use IndexedDB on your device. Local folder links do not upload the folder's contents. Cork has no backend database for your boards and does not send board contents to GitHub.

Optional Google Drive access

If Google Drive is enabled and you choose to connect it, Google handles account selection and consent. Cork requests the Google Drive per-file permission (drive.file) to create board files and read or update files you select for Cork. Cork does not request permission to read your entire Drive.

Cork reads the connected account's display name, email address, and Drive permission identifier to show the connection and check that a saved board belongs to the intended account. The account identifier, Drive file ID, filename, and revision details may be saved locally alongside the board link. Cork keeps access tokens in memory for the current page session, without storing access or refresh tokens in localStorage or IndexedDB. Cork does not receive your Google password.

When you choose a Drive operation, board files transfer directly between your browser and Google. Saved files contain the board's text, positions, appearance, connections, and original photos. Local categories files and local folder permissions are not uploaded with the board. Google's authorization library loads when you open the configured Drive integration; its picker library loads when you choose another Drive file.

Use and sharing of information

Cork uses Google account and file data only to provide the requested connection, file selection, opening, and saving features. Cork does not sell your information, use your content for advertising or AI model training, or send it to an analytics service. Google processes account information and Drive files under its own policies. GitHub Pages and network providers receive ordinary website connection information when serving the static app.

Cork does not change a file's sharing settings. Anyone who already has access to a selected Drive file may see updates you save to it. Manage file sharing through Google Drive.

Cork's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Retention, disconnecting, and deletion

Browser data remains until you remove it or the browser clears it. Clearing this site's browser data removes its locally stored boards, photos, preferences, and file links. Download anything you need first. You can delete exported local files through your device's file manager.

Disconnecting Drive clears Cork's current authorization and asks Google to revoke the grant when Google is reachable. You can also remove Cork's access through your Google account's connected-app settings. Disconnecting does not delete board files or browser copies. Delete Drive board files and manage their trash or revision history in Google Drive.

Contact

For questions or privacy requests, contact the maintainer through the Cork repository. Issues are public; do not include private board content or account credentials.
